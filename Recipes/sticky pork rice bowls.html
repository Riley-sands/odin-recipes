Stick Pork Rice Bowls

Description

a sticky-sweet honey-chili glaze, fluffy white rice, and crunchy garnishes. Whats not too love.

Ingredients

  • 300g pork tenderloin/loin steaks
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ginge
  • 1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 1 tsp sriracha
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp rice vinegar
  • 200g cooked rice
  • 1/2 cup steamed frozen veges
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 1 stalk spring onion

Steps

  1. Cut the pork into bite sized cubes and toss with the cinnamon and ginger and olive oil.
  2. Cook in the airfryer for 8-10 minutes or pan-fry on medium-high heat for 6-8 minutes until lightly browned and cooked through.
  3. While that's cooking, cook your rice according to instructions.
  4. To make the sauce, over a low heat, mix soy sauce, hoisin sauce, sriracha, honey and vinegar and stir until slightly thick and glossy for 1-2 minutes.
  5. Add cooked pork to the sauce and toss until fully coated.
  6. Plate pork on rice with the steam vegetables and garnish with sesame seeds and spring onion.

Spaghetti Bolognese

Description

Spaghetti bolognese is a popular comfort food dish featuring long strands of spaghetti topped with a rich, savory minced meat and tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 Tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 Garlic Clove
  • 1 Onion
  • 500g Beef Mince
  • 1/2 Cup of Red Wine
  • 2 Beef Bouillon Cubes
  • 800g Crushed Tomato
  • 2 Tbsp Tomato paste
  • 2 Tsp White Sugar
  • 2 Tsp Worcestershire Sauce
  • 2 Dried Bay Leaves
  • 2 Sprigs of Fresh Thyme
  • 3/4 Tsp Cooking Salt
  • 1/2 Tsp Black Pepper
  • 400g Dried Spaghetti

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium high heat. Add onion and garlic, cook for 3 minutes or until light golden and softened.
  2. Turn heat up to high and add beef. Cook, breaking it up as your go, until browned.
  3. Add red wine. Bring to simmer and cook for 1 minute, scraping the bottom of the pot, until the alcohol smell is gone.
  4. Add the remaining ingredients. Stir, bring to a simmer then turn down to medium so it bubbles gently. Cook for 20 to 30 minutes (no lid), adding water if the sauce gets too thick for your taste. Stir occasionally.
  5. Taste and add more salt it desired. Serve over spaghetti
